CatchMeUp Support

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 18, 2026

The short version

  • CatchMeUp has no server, analytics, advertising or tracking. We do not receive your recordings, notes or study data.
  • Audio is transcribed on your iPhone.
  • Your content leaves your iPhone only if you turn on iCloud sync, allow an AI provider you chose, or share it yourself.

This policy explains how the CatchMeUp iPhone app ("CatchMeUp", "we" or "us") handles information. If something here is unclear, contact us.

Information stored on your device

CatchMeUp stores the following on your iPhone so the app can work:

  • Audio you record or import, and transcripts of it
  • Notes, titles, meeting agendas and follow-ups
  • Documents, slides and scans you add, and text taken from them
  • Your questions, brain instructions, study questions, typed answers, review times and study schedule
  • Your settings
  • API keys you enter, stored in the iOS Keychain separately for each provider
  • If you sign in, your name, your email and Apple's identifier for you, stored in the iOS Keychain

We cannot access this information. Recap titles and text are also added to your iPhone's Spotlight index so you can search for them.

Information we collect

The app does not send us your content or usage data. It contains no analytics tool, crash reporter, advertising identifier or tracking.

We receive information only if you choose to contact us or send feedback:

  • Email: we receive your address and whatever you write, and use them to reply and improve the app.
  • Send feedback: the app prepares a report and you choose where to send it. The report includes what you write, the app and iOS versions, device model, selected note engine and AI provider, whether iCloud sync is on, a few study settings, and counts such as how many recaps you have. It does not include your notes, transcripts, answers or API keys.

Apple may give us aggregated App Store information, and crash or feedback information from people who choose to share it, under Apple's terms. TestFlight testers also share the information TestFlight provides to developers.

AI providers

You choose how notes are written. The built-in demo and Apple's on-device model do not send your content to an AI provider.

If you use your own API key, CatchMeUp asks for your permission for that provider before sending anything. Supported providers are Anthropic, OpenAI, Google Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter, DeepSeek, Mistral, Together AI, xAI, an Ollama server you specify, and any compatible server you specify.

After you allow a provider, the app may send it, using your key:

  • Transcripts of your recordings and imported audio
  • Recap notes and titles
  • Text from documents, slides and scans you add
  • Meeting agendas
  • Questions you ask a brain, and that brain's instructions
  • Study questions, expected answers and what you type, if answer checking is on

Audio files are not sent to AI providers. The provider you choose handles this text under its own terms and privacy policy, including how long it keeps the text, whether it uses it for training, and where it processes it, which may be outside your country. We do not control this, so please review those terms. You can turn sharing off at any time in Settings, under Data sharing. That stops new sharing but cannot recall text already sent.

iCloud sync

iCloud sync is off by default. If you turn it on, your recordings, including audio, and your notes and study data are stored in your own iCloud Drive and sync to devices using the same Apple Account. Apple handles that data under its own terms.

Sign in with Apple

Signing in is optional. It shows your name in the app and does not create an account with us. Apple provides your identifier, and on your first sign-in may provide your name and email. These stay in your iPhone's Keychain and are not sent to us. You can sign out in Settings. If you stop using Apple sign-in for CatchMeUp in your Apple Account settings, the app removes them.

Device permissions

CatchMeUp asks for access only when a feature needs it, and you can change these choices in iOS Settings:

  • Microphone: to record audio
  • Speech recognition: to transcribe recordings on your iPhone
  • Camera: to scan material you choose to add
  • Reminders: to add follow-ups you choose and avoid adding duplicates
  • Notifications: for study reminders and processing updates, which are created on your iPhone

Recording other people

You are responsible for following the laws and rules that apply to your recordings. Many places require permission from some or all people being recorded. Ask first, and do not use CatchMeUp where recording is not allowed.

Keeping and deleting your information

Your content stays until you delete it.

  • Deleting a recap removes its notes, transcript, audio and questions from this device and from other devices using the same iCloud account if sync is on.
  • Settings, under Storage, can remove audio while keeping notes.
  • Clearing an API key in Settings removes it from the Keychain immediately. You can also revoke the key with its provider.
  • Deleting the app removes its files from your iPhone. iOS can keep Keychain items after an app is deleted, so CatchMeUp erases its saved keys, name, email and sign-in identifier the next time it is installed.
  • Copies in your iCloud Drive remain until you delete them there. Text already sent to an AI provider is governed by that provider.
  • To ask us to delete an email or feedback you sent, contact us.

Your choices and rights

Because your content stays under your control, you can view, change, export or delete most of it in the app. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the use of personal information we hold, which is limited to what you send us. Contact us to make a request. For information held by Apple or an AI provider, contact that company.

We do not sell your personal information or share it for advertising.

Security

API keys and sign-in details are stored in the iOS Keychain. Built-in AI providers are reached over encrypted HTTPS connections. If you enter your own server address, its security depends on that server and connection. No system is perfectly secure, so please protect your device and keys.

Children

CatchMeUp is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page and its date when our practices change. If we start collecting more information, we will update this policy and the app's privacy details first.
