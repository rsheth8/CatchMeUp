Support

Help with the CatchMeUp iPhone app

Need help? Email rahilsheth05@gmail.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version and what happened. Please do not send API keys or private recordings.

How do I try the app without setting anything up?

Open Settings and choose Explore the showcase account. It includes sample recordings, notes, study questions and materials, and it needs no API key.

How does CatchMeUp write my notes?

In Settings, choose who writes the notes. Apple's on-device model is free and private, and it needs a device that supports Apple Intelligence on iOS 26. You can also use your own API key from a supported AI provider. Provider charges, if any, are between you and that provider.

Why does Settings say setup is needed?

If you use an API key, enter a key for the selected provider, then choose Review and Allow Sharing under Data sharing. Each provider has its own key and its own permission.

Is my audio sent to an AI provider?

No. Audio is transcribed on your iPhone. If you allow an AI provider, text such as transcripts and notes is sent to it. If you turn on iCloud sync, audio is stored in your iCloud Drive. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I stop sharing with an AI provider?

Open Settings, go to Data sharing and choose Turn Off. This stops new sharing but cannot recall text already sent.

How do I delete my information?

Delete individual recaps in the app, or remove audio in Settings under Storage. Deleting the app removes its files from your iPhone. Copies in iCloud Drive stay until you delete them there.

Does signing in create an account?

No. Sign in with Apple is optional and only shows your name in the app. You can sign out in Settings.
